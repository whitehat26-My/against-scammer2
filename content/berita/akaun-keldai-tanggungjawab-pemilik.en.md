The offer always looks simple: a company needs a local account for “salary processing” or “customer transactions”, and you are paid a few hundred ringgit a month for doing nothing. The ATM card and banking access are handed over to them.

What happens next is that victims' money flows into an account bearing your name. When a report is filed, the name on that account is yours, and your explanation that you knew nothing has to be made after the account is frozen, not before.

The rule is simple and has no exceptions: do not open a bank account for someone else, do not hand over your ATM card, and do not let your account receive third-party money.
