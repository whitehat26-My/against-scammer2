After a transfer goes through, the money is usually split quickly across several mule accounts and then withdrawn. Every minute that passes shrinks the chance of a freeze landing in time.

That is why the order of your actions matters more than how complete your information is. Call your bank, then 997, before you try to gather every piece of evidence or fully understand what happened. Evidence can be organised later; time cannot be given back.

Calling 997 does not replace a police report. Both are needed: 997 for the immediate response, the police report so an investigation can proceed.
