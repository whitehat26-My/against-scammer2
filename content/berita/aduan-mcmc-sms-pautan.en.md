Most people delete a scam SMS and get on with their day. That is understandable, but the same message is going out to thousands of other people, and some of them will click.

When you file a complaint, include the sender number or sender ID, the date and time, and the full link from the message — write the link so it cannot be clicked, for example `hxxps://contoh-tipu[.]xyz`. Screenshots help.

If you already entered card details or an OTP, an MCMC complaint is not the first step. Call your bank and 997 first, then file the complaint.
