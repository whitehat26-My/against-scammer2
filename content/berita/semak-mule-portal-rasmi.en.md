Semak Mule works from police reports that have already been filed. It answers one question, but an important one: has this number appeared in a reported fraud case?

Two things to understand about the result. First, a “clean” number is not proof of safety — syndicates open new accounts and numbers every day, and an account that has just started being used has not yet been reported by anyone. Second, if the number does appear, stop there. Do not negotiate, do not ask for an explanation.

This portal points you to Semak Mule on every check result, for every kind of detail. We keep no copy of that database.
